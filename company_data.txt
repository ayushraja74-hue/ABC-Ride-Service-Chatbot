
Company Name: ABC Ride Service

About Company:
ABC Ride Service is a fictional taxi booking company.
It provides transportation services to customers.

Service Cities:
- Delhi
- Noida
- Gurugram

Ride Types:
- Personal Ride
- Shared Ride
- Airport Ride

Booking Process:
Customers can book a ride using the ABC Ride Service mobile application.

Cancellation Policy:
Customers can cancel their booking before a driver is assigned.
After driver assignment, a cancellation fee of Rs. 30 applies.

Payment Methods:
- Cash
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card

Customer Support:
Customer support is available from 9 AM to 8 PM every day.

Driver Assignment:
The system assigns an available driver based on the customer's pickup location.

Airport Ride:
Airport rides are available in Delhi and Gurugram.

Refund Policy:
Eligible refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days.
